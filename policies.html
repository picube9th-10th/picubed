πcubed policies

Last updated: 16 November 2026. πcubed sells digital Maths study material (question banks, test papers and solutions) for Class 9 and Class 10.

Privacy policy

You don't need an account. We keep very little information:

  • Library code and device ID. When you buy something we give you a library code. Your browser stores that code and a random device ID so we can show your purchases and limit each code to 2 devices. We do not know who you are from these.
  • Purchase records. We store what was bought, the amount, the Razorpay order and payment IDs, and the library code.
  • Payments. Payments are handled by Razorpay. We never see or store your card or UPI details. Razorpay may collect your phone number or email at checkout, and we may see these in our Razorpay dashboard for support purposes.
  • Children. The site is made for students. Payments should be made by a parent or guardian.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising. Our service providers are Supabase (database and file storage), GitHub (website hosting) and Razorpay (payments). To ask us to delete the records linked to your library code, contact us below.

Terms of use

  • All prices are in Indian rupees (INR). The price is shown before you pay.
  • Purchased PDFs are for your own study. You may not copy, resell, upload or share them, or share your library code with others.
  • Each library code works on up to 2 devices. We may block a code that is used in a way that breaks these terms.
  • Access is delivered online right after payment. Keep your library code safe: it is how you get your purchases back on another device.
  • The free quizzes and free question banks are provided as they are. We try to keep our content accurate but cannot guarantee it is free of mistakes.

Refund policy

All sales are final. We do not offer refunds on digital products once they have been purchased, because the material is delivered immediately and cannot be returned.

The only exceptions are problems on our side. If you were charged but could not access your purchase, or you were charged twice for the same item, contact us within 7 days with your payment ID and we will fix it or refund the duplicate payment.

Before you buy, you can try the free quizzes and the free question banks in chapters 1 to 3 to see the quality of our material.
